Tug of Ages

Three armies from three different ages. One battlefield. Build your army, send it out in waves, and push the front line all the way to the enemy castle.

Coming soon to the App Store

Choose your army

SoldiersRiflemen, machine gunners, flamethrowers, snipers, tanks and helicopters.
DinosaursAn acid-spitting Dilophosaurus, Pentaceratops, Ankylosaurus, Apatosaurus, T-rex and Pteranodon.
WizardsA wizard, a giant swinging a tree, and four elemental dragons.

How it plays

  • Place units in your build zone with the money you earn.
  • Every wave, a copy of your army marches out and fights on its own.
  • Counter light, heavy and flying units, upgrade key troops, and break the enemy's line.
  • Play 1v1, 2v2 or 3v3 against the computer, with computer allies at your side.
