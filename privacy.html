Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

The short version: Tug of Ages has no accounts, no ads and no in-app purchases. Your settings and records stay on your device. The game sends anonymous gameplay statistics, which are never linked to you, never used to track you, and can be switched off in Settings.

What stays on your device

Your settings (sound, haptics, graphics, frame rate and so on), your win and loss record, and your career statistics are saved only on your device. We never receive them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Anonymous usage statistics

To learn which parts of the game people enjoy and where it runs poorly, Tug of Ages sends anonymous events to TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service. An event includes:

  • What happened in the game: for example a match starting or ending (with the army, mode, result, duration, units placed, upgrades and frame rate), the tutorial being closed, a menu being opened, or a setting being changed.
  • Technical details: device model, iOS version, screen size, app version and build, the device's language setting, and whether the app came from TestFlight or the App Store.
  • Simple usage counts: the date of your first session, the number of sessions and the number of days you have played.
  • An anonymous install ID: a random number created when the game is first opened. It is scrambled (hashed) on your device before it is sent, and it is not tied to your Apple ID, your name, your email, your location, or any other app.

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, precise or approximate location, or advertising identifier. This data is not linked to your identity, is not used for tracking or advertising, and is not sold or shared with data brokers. We only look at it in aggregate, as charts and totals.

Turning it off

Open Settings → Controls in the game and set Anonymous usage data to Off. The game then stops sending events and discards any that have not been sent yet.

Children

Tug of Ages does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. The anonymous statistics described above contain nothing that identifies a person.

Changes

If a future version adds features that change what is collected (for example purchases), this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
