How to play

Pick an army (Soldiers, Dinosaurs or Wizards) and a mode (1v1, 2v2 or 3v3), then press Play. Spend your money on units in your build zone. At every wave, a copy of your whole army marches down the lane and fights on its own. Push the front line to the enemy castle and destroy it to win. The first battle opens a short field guide; you can read it again any time in Settings → How to play.

Tips

  • Counters matter. Anti-armor units beat heavy ones, rapid fire shreds light units, and only some units can hit flyers. The Army Collection shows what each unit is good against.
  • Upgrades make a unit tougher or stronger for the rest of the match.
  • Bank depots cost money up front but raise your income for the rest of the match.
  • The camera is free: drag with one finger to orbit, pinch to zoom, drag with two fingers to pan.

Frequently asked

The game runs hot or slow on my device.

Open Settings → Graphics and choose a lighter preset, or set the frame limit to 30 FPS.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No. Tug of Ages has no accounts and plays fully offline.

How do I turn off usage statistics?

Open Settings → Controls and set Anonymous usage data to Off. See the privacy policy for what it covers.

How do I reset my record?

Your wins, losses and statistics are kept on your device. Deleting the app removes them.
